Privacy Policy & Player Support

Last updated: 16 September 2026
Android application: com.gaha.vrenthal

About this policy

This policy applies to the offline Android version of Vrenthal, a first-person zombie survival game. No account or login is required. Contact us at skolasoft@gmail.com for privacy questions or game support.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings, language, touch-control layout, local profile progress, earned cosmetic unlocks, match records and preparation checkpoints in its private storage on your device. These records are used to restore your preferences and progress. They are not sent to the developer.

The game reads device performance and battery or thermal information to adjust rendering and frame rate. Optional gyroscope input is used to assist aiming when you enable it. These readings stay on the device and are not transmitted. The game does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

No in-game collection or tracking

This version has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated server connection, online multiplayer, chat, account system or in-app purchases. The game does not collect or share personal data off your device. It does not sell your information or track you across apps. Purchases inside a match use only virtual cash earned by playing, with no real-money purchase option.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall without retaining its data. To erase them, use Android Settings → Apps → Vrenthal → Storage → Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This permanently removes local progress. The developer cannot access or restore those files. Device backup or restoration features, if enabled by your operating system, are governed by your device provider's settings and policy.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and satisfy applicable legal obligations.

External services and links

Google Play handles distribution, store transactions if any, security and related device services under Google's policies. Opening this privacy page or a source link in the credits launches an external browser. Those websites process connection information under their own policies. GitHub Pages may process an IP address and standard connection data to serve this page. This page contains no analytics, advertisements, forms or tracking cookies.

Audience

Vrenthal is intended for adults, not children. It contains horror themes, violence involving human-like undead, weapons and blood effects. Regional age ratings are determined through the store's content-rating process.

Changes

If a future version adds online services, ads, purchases or different data practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The current revision date appears above.
